## 摘要摘录

Language modeling has shifted in recent years from a distribution over strings to prediction models with textual inputs and outputs for general-purpose tasks. This position paper highlights the often overlooked implications of this shift for the use of large language models (LLMs) as probability estimators, especially for world probabilities.
