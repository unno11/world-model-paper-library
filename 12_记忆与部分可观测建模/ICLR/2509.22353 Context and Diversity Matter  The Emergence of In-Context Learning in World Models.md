## 摘要摘录

The capability of predicting environmental dynamics underpins both biological neural systems and general embodied AI in adapting to their surroundings. Yet prevailing approaches rest on static world models that falter when confronted with novel or rare configurations. We investigate in-context learning (ICL) of world models, shifting attention from zero-shot performance to the growth and asymptotic limits of the world model.
