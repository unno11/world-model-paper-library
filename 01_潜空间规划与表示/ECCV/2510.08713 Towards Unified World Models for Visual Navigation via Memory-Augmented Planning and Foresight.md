## 摘要摘录

Enabling embodied agents to imagine future states is essential for robust and generalizable visual navigation. Yet, state-of-the-art systems typically rely on modular designs that decouple navigation planning from visual world modeling, which often induces state-action misalignment and weak adaptability in novel or dynamic scenarios.
