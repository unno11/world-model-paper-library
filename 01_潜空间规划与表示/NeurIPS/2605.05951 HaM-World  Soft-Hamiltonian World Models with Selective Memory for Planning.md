## 摘要摘录

World models support model-based planning through learned latent dynamics, but imagined rollouts can become unstable as the planning horizon grows or the dynamics distribution shifts. We propose HaM-World, a structured world model that combines history-conditioned selective memory with a Soft-Hamiltonian latent dynamics prior.
