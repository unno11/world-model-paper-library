## 摘要摘录

Augmenting vision-language-action models (VLAs) with world models is promising for robotic policy learning but faces challenges in jointly predicting states and actions due to the modality gap. To address this, we propose DUal-STream diffusion (DUST), a world-model augmented VLA framework featuring a multimodal diffusion transformer that maintains separate modality streams while enabling cross-modal knowledge sharing.
