## 摘要摘录

Text- and image-conditioned world generators can create visually rich 3D environments, yet these worlds often remain silent or rely on soundtracks synthesized solely from text or rendered video. Although such audio can convey what should be heard, it lacks an explicit representation of where sound sources are located and how their perceived sound should vary with listener movement.
