## 摘要摘录

Vision-based legged locomotion methods assume clean depth at training time and rely on hand-tuned post-processing filters at deployment. However, filter parameters are rarely disclosed, hindering reproducibility, and performance degrades substantially when depth noise is left unaddressed.
