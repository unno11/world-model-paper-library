## 摘要摘录

Video world models aim to simulate dynamic, real-world environments, yet existing methods struggle to provide unified and precise control over camera and multi-object motion, as videos inherently capture dynamics in the projected 2D image plane. To bridge this gap, we introduce VerseCrafter, a geometry-driven video world model that generates dynamic, realistic videos from a unified 4D geometric world state.
