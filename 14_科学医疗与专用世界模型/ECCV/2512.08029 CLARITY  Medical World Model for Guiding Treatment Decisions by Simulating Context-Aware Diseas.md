## 摘要摘录

Clinical decision-making in oncology requires forecasting how disease evolves under treatment, yet most AI systems remain static predictors that cannot model longitudinal, treatment-conditioned progression. Although generative and world models have demonstrated strong capabilities in general domains, their adaptation to medicine remains limited and insufficient for capturing complex, treatment-induced physiological dynamics across temporal scales.
