## 摘要摘录

We present TriO, a multi-modal unsupervised world model that predicts 4D occupancy, obstacle segmentation, flow and LiDAR. In contrast to prior work, TriO utilizes three distinct sensor modalities (camera, LiDAR, and RADAR) as both inputs and sources of self-supervision, eliminating the need for additional human annotations.
