## 摘要摘录

A robust world model must strike the balance between faithfully capturing environmental dynamics and abstracting away from irrelevant content. While reconstruction-based world models ensure faithful supervision, they misallocate representational capacity by pixel area rather than dynamics relevance for visual tasks, which can cause task-irrelevant content to dominate the learned representation.
