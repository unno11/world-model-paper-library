## 摘要摘录

Developing generalist systems that retain human-like data efficiency is a central challenge. While world models (WMs) offer a promising path, existing research often conflates architectural mechanisms with the independent impact of model \emph{scale}. In this work, we use a minimalist transformer world model to analyze scaling behaviors on the Atari 100k benchmark, using fixed offline datasets derived from a presupposed expert policy.
