## 摘要摘录

Equipping VLM agents with world modeling capabilities has shown strong potential for complex reasoning and long-horizon planning, while reducing the dependence of policy learning on costly real-world interactions. Existing methods mainly rely on prospective simulation to predict the consequences of candidate actions. However, this forward-only paradigm focuses on what will happen next and provides limited constraints for verifying whether an action is causally consistent…
